Your Plan at a Glance
Healthy Weight Gain
Goal: gain 8–12 kg over 6 months. Small calorie surplus (+300 kcal/day) combined with strength training. No crash diets, no rapid changes — slow and steady wins.
6-Day Split, 3 Phases
Train Mon–Sat, rest Sunday. Weeks 1–2: warm-ups and mobility only. Weeks 3–8: warm-up then strength. Weeks 9–12: heavier, progressive. Every session starts with a warm-up.
Progressive Overload
Every 2–3 weeks, add a little more weight or one more rep. This is how muscles grow. Do not rush it — small increases compound dramatically over 12 weeks.
Sleep & Recovery
Muscles grow during sleep, not during workouts. 7–8 hours every night is part of your training plan. Skimping on sleep literally slows your results — it is not optional.
